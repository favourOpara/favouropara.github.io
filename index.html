02 / Security operations

An alert fires. Here is what happens next.

Triage is a discipline, not a reflex. This is the loop I work, from the moment a detection lands in the queue to the rule change that stops it coming back.

  1. 01

    Detect

    Alerts surface from Sentinel, QRadar and InsightIDR on the log side, and from CrowdStrike Falcon and Defender for Endpoint on the endpoint side. Threat intelligence feeds sharpen the correlation logic so real signals arrive earlier.

  2. 02

    Triage

    Severity, scope and blast radius first. Then the false positive question, checked against the SOP rather than a hunch. Anything that survives that filter gets an owner and a clock.

  3. 03

    Investigate

    Pivot across endpoint, network and identity telemetry. Correlate indicators of compromise between data sources, reconstruct the timeline, and separate what the attacker did from what the environment does normally.

  4. 04

    Contain and escalate

    Escalate with evidence attached, not adjectives. Recommend containment that the business can actually absorb, and brief the stakeholders who have to authorise it.

  5. 05

    Document and tune

    Write the incident record while it's fresh. Then fix the detection: refine the correlation rule, cut the false positives, and feed the root cause back into the risk register so the gap closes permanently.
